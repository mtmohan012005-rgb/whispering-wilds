# Key Game Features

- **Seamless Open World Traversal**: Explore eight distinct biocultural zones without loading screens, powered by background chunk streaming.
- **Combat-Free Exploration**: A peaceful narrative adventure emphasizing observation, environmental investigation, and regional puzzles.
- **Living Cultural World**: Daily temple rituals, morning Kolam drawings, bustling Kadai tea stalls, and vibrant harvest festivals.
- **Wildlife & Photography**: Observe and catalog indigenous species like the Nilgiri Tahr, Lion-tailed Macaque, and Malabar Giant Squirrel using a dedicated 3D Photo Mode.
- **Full Controller Support**: Complete plug-and-play support for Xbox and PlayStation gamepads with dynamic glyph adaptation.
- **Bilingual Experience**: Authored in full native Tamil and English across all UI, dialogue, and journals.
- **Ethical Single-Player Game**: Zero microtransactions, zero loot boxes, zero pay-to-win mechanics. Complete offline playability.
