# Accessibility Features Disclosure

- **Visual & UI**:
  - UI Scaling options (100%, 125%, 150%)
  - High Contrast mode for enhanced text readability
  - Reduced Motion setting to minimize camera shake and dynamic bobbing
  - Screen Flash reduction: Photo flash and lightning intensity can be disabled
- **Audio & Subtitles**:
  - Full Subtitles for all dialogue and ambient field recordings
  - Speaker name tags and regional dialect indicators
  - Independent audio sliders: Master, Ambient Nature, Music, Voice, Sound Effects
- **Gameplay & Traversal**:
  - Combat-free design eliminates reaction-time penalties
  - Fully remappable keys for both keyboard and gamepad
  - Toggle vs Hold options for sprint and camera locks
