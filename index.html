The Whispering Wilds

காட்டு வழி • தடம்

An open-world narrative exploration and investigation game set across the diverse landscapes of Tamil Nadu, India. Explore Chennai's George Town, Pichavaram mangrove wetlands, Thanjavur's Cauvery delta, Chettinad heritage architecture, Mamallapuram's coastline, and the Nilgiris mountain sanctuary.

Built with Unity 6 and HDRP, featuring bilingual English-Tamil localization, NPC dialogue, investigation quests, and a connected campaign spanning six regions.

System Requirements

  • Windows 10/11 x64
  • Unity 6 (6000.6.3f1) for development
  • HDRP 17.7.0 render pipeline
  • DirectX 11/12 compatible GPU
  • 4 GB RAM minimum
